# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Technical developers evaluating WitnessFitness in a live presentation. They understand APIs and smart contracts but may not know Reclaim, Compact, or the project's trust model. They need to follow the architecture without losing the central problem.

## Product Purpose

Explain how WitnessFitness converts an untrusted browser-supplied API value into a threshold-attested claim that a Midnight contract can verify. Success means the audience understands the HTTPS limitation, Reclaim's role, the 2-of-3 notary bridge, the privacy boundary, and what Midnight adds before the live demo begins.

## Brand Personality

Precise, transparent, kinetic. The voice is technically confident, conversational, and candid about trust assumptions and current privacy limitations.

## Anti-references

Avoid the visual style of the existing WitnessFitness pitch deck. Avoid generic startup cards, decorative dashboards, tiny repeated labels, excessive amber-on-black styling, ornamental crypto imagery, and slides that resemble documentation pasted onto a canvas.

## Design Principles

- Make the architecture legible before making it impressive.
- Show each trust transition as a concrete change in what can be verified.
- Use progressive disclosure so the audience always knows where it is in the flow.
- State privacy boundaries and future work without marketing overclaims.
- Give every slide one spoken job and one dominant visual idea.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast, keep essential meaning independent of color, maintain projection-safe type sizes, support keyboard navigation, and provide reduced-motion behavior.
